About Winfred

Helping others heal & grow

"I believe that every person deserves access to support that helps them thrive — emotionally, mentally, and socially."

My name is Winnie Mutio — a graduate of Bachelor of Arts in Counseling Psychology from Pan Africa Christian University, a journey that began in 2023. Choosing this course was one of the most significant decisions of my life, because it aligns deeply with my passion for helping others overcome challenges and achieve psychological and emotional wellbeing.

My inspiration came from observing the emotional, social, and mental health struggles faced by people in my community — individuals dealing with stress, anxiety, family conflicts, and low self-esteem, yet often without professional support. That gap is what drove me to pursue this path.

Balancing coursework, family, and personal responsibilities was not easy, but those experiences only strengthened my determination and prepared me for the professional journey ahead. I aspire to become a compassionate counseling psychologist — supporting individuals, families, and communities in overcoming life's challenges and building meaningful lives.

I am grateful to God who carried me through every high and low of this program, and to my family whose love and support made this achievement possible.
